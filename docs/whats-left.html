A few quick ones

Need your OK

Your tab list did not include one, and you put the contact details on the home page instead. That is the normal pattern: most practices use a standing button rather than a tab, because a button that says what it does converts better than a link that says “Contact”.

Nothing is buried. Schedule Free Consultation sits in the header on every page, the contact block with the form and the map is at the bottom of every page, and the footer carries your phone, address and hours on every page too. Nobody has to hunt.

The one thing worth watching: we took the phone number out of the header to free the slot for Patient FAQ. It is still in the footer and the contact block on every page, so it is never more than a scroll away — but if you would rather have it back up top, say so and the FAQ tab can go somewhere else.
