Choosing the heading font

These are the big headings only — the body text stays exactly as it is. Each block shows real headings from your site, and the bottom line isolates the letters worth looking at: capital S, lowercase f, and the fi pair.

What everyone else uses

We looked at what other orthodontic sites do. Great Lakes Bay Orthodontics uses Proxima Nova, Invisalign uses Roboto, and the American Association of Orthodontists uses Montserrat — all plain sans-serif, and all fairly similar to each other.

That is the argument for the serif you have now: it looks like a practice rather than a corporation, and it is the reason your site does not blend in with theirs. The only issue is that Fraunces draws some letters unusually. The options below keep the warmth but draw them normally. The last one shows what the sans-serif route would look like, for comparison.

If you want a recommendation

Source Serif 4 or Lora. Both keep the warm, human feel you said you liked, and both draw a normal S and f. Lora is slightly softer, Source Serif 4 slightly cleaner. Either is a small change — nothing else about the site moves.
