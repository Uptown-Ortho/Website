Meet your doctor

Dr. Brandan LeBourdais

DDS, MS — Orthodontist
American Association of Orthodontists · Great Lakes Association of Orthodontists · American Dental Association · Michigan Dental Association · Saginaw Valley District Dental Society

Dr. LeBourdais offers over 25 years of experience in the dental community, from Bay City to Okinawa, Japan — treating patients ranging from children and adults to Marine Special Forces and Navy S.E.A.L. Teams. As a former Navy general dentist, he was honored with the "Top Doc" award during his service with the 3rd Dental Battalion in Okinawa. Outside the office, he's usually camping, traveling, or watching a game with his wife and two sons — and the family stays involved locally, supporting area youth sports, arts and music programs, and local shelters.

"Bay City is my hometown, and it is a privilege to build my practice here."
Meet your doctor

Dr. Hannah Collison

DDS, MS — Orthodontist
American Association of Orthodontists · Great Lakes Association of Orthodontists · American Dental Association · Michigan Dental Association · Saginaw Valley District Dental Society · Northern Michigan Orthodontic Study Club

Originally from Saginaw, Dr. Collison's own experience as an orthodontic patient sparked a lifelong commitment to helping others achieve confident smiles. She earned her Bachelor's in Biomedical Sciences from Central Michigan University, then her DDS from the University of Detroit Mercy, where she was selected for their Orthodontic Program to complete her Master's and Certificate in Orthodontics. Outside the practice, she's spending time with her husband Jason and their two kids, Stevie and Cal — or trying a new restaurant with the latest NYT bestseller in hand.

"I'm thrilled to be part of your orthodontic journey."
