About Us

The people behind your smile.

Two orthodontists, one team, and a practice that has been part of Bay City for over twenty years.

Meet your doctor

Dr. Brandan LeBourdais

DDS, MS — Orthodontist
American Association of Orthodontists · Great Lakes Association of Orthodontists · American Dental Association · Michigan Dental Association · Saginaw Valley District Dental Society

With over 25 years of experience in dentistry, Dr. LeBourdais brings a unique perspective to orthodontic care. His career has taken him from Bay City to Okinawa, Japan, where he served as a Navy general dentist with the 3rd Dental Battalion and was honored with the “Top Doc” award for his clinical abilities, leadership, and dedication to service.

Dr. LeBourdais believes every patient deserves individualized care and a treatment plan designed around their unique needs and goals. His comprehensive approach focuses on creating not only a beautiful smile, but one that supports long-term dental health.

Outside the office, Dr. LeBourdais enjoys spending time with his wife and two sons. As a family, they love camping, traveling, golf, skiing, and supporting the local schools, sports, arts, and community organizations they’re proud to call home.

"As a Bay City native, I'm honored to serve the community that helped shape me."
Meet your doctor

Dr. Hannah Collison

DDS, MS — Orthodontist
American Association of Orthodontists · Great Lakes Association of Orthodontists · American Dental Association · Michigan Dental Association · Saginaw Valley District Dental Society · Northern Michigan Orthodontic Study Club

Originally from Saginaw, Michigan, Dr. Hannah Collison’s passion for orthodontics began with her own experience as a patient. Today, she is dedicated to helping others achieve confident smiles, healthy teeth, and lasting function.

Dr. Collison earned her Bachelor’s degree in Biomedical Sciences from Central Michigan University (Fire Up, Chips!), followed by her DDS from the University of Detroit Mercy. She went on to complete her Master’s of Science and Certificate in Orthodontics through UDM’s prestigious orthodontic program.

She believes great orthodontic care goes beyond straight teeth, focusing on a healthy, functional bite and long-term dental health.

Outside the office, Dr. Collison enjoys spending time with her husband, Jason, and their two children, Stevie and Cal. She loves trying new restaurants, reading, and making memories with family and friends.

"I love helping patients gain confidence through a smile they're proud to share."
